Formula Sheet

ρ = m / V
kg/m³ — density
γ = ρg
N/m³ — specific weight, g ≈ 9.81 m/s²
SG = ρfluid / ρwater
dimensionless — specific gravity
v = V / m = 1 / ρ
m³/kg — specific volume
ν = μ / ρ
m²/s — kinematic viscosity
K = −V (dP / dV)
Pa — bulk modulus
h = 2γ cosθ / (ρ g r)
m — capillary rise (extension, source to confirm)
