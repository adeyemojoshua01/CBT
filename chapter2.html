These properties control how every fluid behaves.

Specific Weight and Specific Gravity

Specific weight is weight per volume. Specific gravity compares density to water.

Objects float or sink according to SG.

γ = ρg    SG = ρfluid / ρwater

Newtonian vs Non-Newtonian

Apply shear and watch the shear stress respond. Water is linear. Ketchup thins. Toothpaste has a yield stress.

Shear stress vs rate of deformation.

Do not assume every liquid is Newtonian.

Temperature and Viscosity

Heat a liquid and it flows more easily. Heat a gas and it becomes more viscous. The effects are opposite.

Left: liquid layers. Right: gas particles.

Temperature has opposite effects on liquid and gas viscosity.

Compressibility and Bulk Modulus

Compress air and water with the same pressure. Only the air changes volume visibly.

Left: air syringe. Right: water syringe.

K = −V (dP / dV)
Pa
Large K = hard to compress (water, 2.2 GPa). Small K = easy (air).
Neglect compressibility for most liquid flows and low-speed gas flows below ~30% of the speed of sound.
